Featured · SQL analysis

OC Transpo Service Analysis

PostgreSQLSQLPythonpgAdminGit

How is Ottawa's bus and train service spread across the city and the week? A Python script loads the GTFS feed into PostgreSQL, and ten SQL queries answer questions about frequency, busy stops, weekend cuts and late-night gaps. The transit lab above runs on its results.

How I built it
  • One-step loader: 175 routes, 5,833 stops, 94,115 trips and about 3.8 million stop times.
  • Converted GTFS times past 24:00 to seconds since midnight so they sort correctly.
  • Wrote a service_ids_on(date) function to handle the weekly calendar plus holiday exceptions.
  • Estimated missing stop times by spacing them between known stops.
  • Used LAG() for the gap between buses and FILTER to count weekday vs weekend trips in one pass; left out school routes that skewed the averages.
